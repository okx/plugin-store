## Overview
PolyOracle Sentiment is an AI-driven prediction market agent. It analyzes social sentiment and whale signals to help users place high-confidence bets on Polymarket events.

## Prerequisites
- OKX Wallet with Agentic Wallet enabled.
- USDC or POL on Polygon (for Polymarket betting).

## Quick Start
1. Ask: "Analyze sentiment for [Market Slug] on PolyOracle."
2. The agent will show a confidence score.
3. Say "Bet on this" to execute the trade via Polymarket.
