## Overview
Cross-Hedge Alpha is an advanced risk management strategy that bridges Hyperliquid and Polymarket. It protects your perp positions from liquidation or major drawdowns using correlated prediction market events.

## Prerequisites
- OKX Wallet with Agentic Wallet enabled.
- Assets on both Arbitrum (HL) and Polygon (Poly).

## Quick Start
1. Run "onchainos hedge auto" to scan your portfolio.
2. Review the suggested Polymarket hedge.
3. Confirm to execute the multi-protocol protection.
